scores; most cells listed here carry the reported statistics only, because

`extract_predictions.py` and is the authoritative list — the build fails if
a cell declared here turns out to have scores after all, or if a cell
declared to have scores does not.

## FaceShifter, shipped recipe — the June evaluation's own per-video scores (the table's printed interval and P)

The shipped-recipe FaceShifter cells were first evaluated in the June out-of-distribution follow-up, before the per-video-prediction convention reached that path: the driver was ad-hoc and saved no predictions (this is audit defect D8). In revision 1 the same frozen checkpoints (EMA weights, the published selection) were re-scored post hoc on the same 280 videos, and those per-video scores ARE released, in `predictions/faceshifter/` beside the backbone's; they reproduce the table's published AUCs and deltas to three decimals and give them their fourth. What is not released, because it was never saved, is the June run's own output — so the printed 95% interval and P(delta>0), which come from the June bootstrap draw and are kept as published, are recorded rather than recomputed.

## What it would take to close these

Regenerating per-video scores for any cell above needs a forward pass of the
released checkpoint over that evaluation set's face crops. Crops are not
redistributable; they are deterministically
regenerable from agreement-obtained source videos plus the per-video crop
coordinates in the Zenodo deposit (see the README). No re-training is involved.

Prediction files in this release: **776**.
